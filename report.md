## Methodology
We merged Hyperliquid trading data with Bitcoin Fear/Greed sentiment using date alignment. Key metrics such as PnL, win rate, and trade size were analyzed.

## Insights
- Greed phases show highest profitability and win rate
- Fear phases show lower returns but higher risk behavior
- Traders adjust position size based on sentiment

## Strategy Recommendations
- Reduce leverage during Fear periods
- Increase position sizing during Greed with caution
- Focus on consistent trading behavior in volatile markets

## Conclusion
Market sentiment significantly impacts trader performance and behavior, making it a useful factor for trading strategy design.
